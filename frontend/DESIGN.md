## Brand & Style

This design system targets ultra-high-net-worth individuals, family offices, and institutional portfolio directors who demand rigorous computational intelligence framed by discreet wealth management heritage. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Luxury Minimalism** integrated with **Swiss Editorial Precision**:
- Atmospheric purity: Expansive ivory negative space conveys clarity, deliberate restraint, and calm authority over frantic market noise.
- Monastic discipline: High structural alignment, hairline linear dividers, and sharp rectangularity communicate architectural permanence.
- Algorithmic elegance: Subtle champagne ambient accents signify state-of-the-art predictive computation without defaulting to synthetic, juvenile neon tropes.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sensation of institutional pedigree through a warm, archival base and razor-sharp typographic contrast:

- **Primary (`#0A0A0A`):** Obsidian black. Governs all foundational typography, dominant structured iconography, and pure binary states.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C9A45C`):** Muted champagne gold. Reserved for institutional validation indicators, premium focus states, selected tab underlines, and key predictive delta metrics.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#E0C078`):** Pale radiant gold. Applied to microscopic glow highlights, hover illumination boundaries, and ambient state transitions.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F6F2`):** Warm unbleached ivory. Serves as the global backdrop to negate cold clinical sterility and evoke tactile archival stationery.
- **Structural Outlines:** `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)` for systemic dividers; `rgba(201, 164, 92, 0.22)` for elevated or selected AI data containers.
- **Semantic Accents:** Subdued British Racing Green (`#1B4D3E`) for positive yields; Deep Oxblood (`#5C1D24`) for asset vulnerabilities and negative yield indicators.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy pairs an authoritative editorial serif with two functional sans-serif engines:

- **Editorial Serifs (Newsreader):** Carries historical prestige, bespoke private banking character, and human intellect. Utilized exclusively for macro valuations, portfolio summary headlines, and narrative market syntheses.
- **Neo-Grotesque Workhorse (Hanken Grotesk):** Provides hyper-legible, neutral, and unembellished readability for transactional tables, contextual body copy, and navigation structures.
- **Monospaced Technical Precision (JetBrains Mono):** Mandated for tabular financial feeds, algorithmic confidence scores, asset tickers, and time-series metadata. Enforces rigorous alignment across high-frequency fiscal matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses a strictly disciplined 12-column Swiss grid model grounded in consistent visual rhythms:

- **Grid Geometry:** 12 columns desktop (1280px+ breakpoint) with `1.5rem` gutters and dynamic outer margins scaling up to `5rem` on ultra-wide viewports; 8 columns on tablet (768px-1279px) with `1.5rem` gutters; 4 columns on mobile (<768px) with `1rem` gutters.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Vertical progression is regulated in multiples of `8px`. Dense financial telemetry uses `space-xs` and `space-sm` for unified cohesion, whereas structural editorial section breaks consume `space-xl` and higher to protect white space.
- **Framing & Density:** Elements are framed inside hairline borders rather than heavy container blocks, prioritizing raw whitespace over graphic enclosure.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects heavy, artificial drop shadows in favor of low-contrast hairline borders, subtle tonal layering, and directional champagne illumination:

- **Hairline Outlines:** Surfaces are demarcated by 1px borders using `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)` on neutral ivory canvas, maintaining architectural lightness.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Default canvas sits on `#F7F6F2`. Active analytical workspaces and modal overlays shift subtly to `#FFFFFF`. Embedded data readouts and inset metrics drop slightly to `#EFECE4`.
- **Atmospheric Gold Ambient Bloom:** Focus states, active AI reasoning sequences, and prioritized intelligence alerts emit a restrained aura: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(201, 164, 92, 0.16)`.
- **Card Elevation:** Deep shadows are strictly forbidden. Hover states transition the 1px hairline border from neutral obsidian-alpha to `rgba(201, 164, 92, 0.45)` with a faint vertical offset shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a sharp geometric posture (`roundedness: 0` / `0px` border-radius globally):

- Buttons, card modules, table rows, input boundaries, and modals maintain pure 90-degree right angles.
- This rectilinear profile reinforces institutional gravitas, precision finance, and printing-press lineage.
- Circles are permitted exclusively for operational status dots, active AI pulse indicators, and user avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Obsidian `#0A0A0A` background, warm ivory `#F7F6F2` text, 0px border-radius, `label-lg` uppercase typography. Active/Hover: shifts background to `#1E1E1E` with a 1px border highlight of `#C9A45C`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background, 1px border `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.20)`, obsidian text. Hover: border shifts to `#0A0A0A` with ivory shift to `#EFECE4`.
- **Tertiary / Text:** Borderless, `0A0A0A` text, with an animated hairline underline expanding from 0% to 100% width on hover in `#C9A45C`.

### Chips & Badges
- 0px border radius, 1px border `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.12)`, padded with `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally.
- Uses `label-md` (JetBrains Mono).
- AI Confidence chips utilize a background of `rgba(201, 164, 92, 0.08)` and border of `rgba(201, 164, 92, 0.35)` with `#0A0A0A` text.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Minimalist ledger styling: no vertical column lines. 
- Row dividers are 1px borders of `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.06)`. 
- Header rows utilize `label-md` uppercase text tracking at `0.08em` in `#5A5A57`.
- Hovering a row highlights the entire row with a tonal wash of `#F0EEE7` and exposes an end-cap hairline in `#C9A45C`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat ivory fields framed by a crisp 1px border of `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.16)`.
- Typographic input set in `body-md` (Hanken Grotesk).
- Focus state: border activates to `#0A0A0A` accompanied by an ambient champagne highlight glow (`0 0 0 1px #C9A45C`). No standard browser focus rings.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Hard-edged 12px boxes (`roundedness: 0`).
- Unchecked: 1px border `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.25)` over pure ivory.
- Checked: `#0A0A0A` solid fill with a minimalist centered square in `#C9A45C`.

### Wealth Intelligence Cards
- Unfilled `#FFFFFF` or tonal ivory `#FAF9F6` backgrounds enclosed by 1px borders of `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)`.
- Header consists of a monospaced identifier badge juxtaposed against an editorial serif metric.
- Dynamic predictive charts inside cards use hairline 1.25px curves traced in `#C9A45C` over an ultra-faint gradient drop.
